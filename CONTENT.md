## Copy rules for this site

1. **Say the specific thing.** "Certified biomedical technicians" beats "quality service."
2. **Numbers or nothing.** "24/7 support" is a claim. "We answer emergency calls in under 30 minutes" is a promise. Only write the promise if you keep it.
3. **Short sentences.** Your readers are procurement officers and clinicians reading on a phone between patients.
4. **One call to action per section.** Two competing buttons halve both.
5. **No stock-photo language.** Cut "cutting-edge", "world-class", "state-of-the-art", "solutions-driven", "seamlessly". They signal nothing.
